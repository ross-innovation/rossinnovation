# Ross Innovation

**Robotics, unconventionally.**

Ross Innovation Ltd is a UK robotics company developing novel robotic manipulation technologies for confined, complex and hazardous environments.

Our principal technology, **Re-Vector®**, introduces a fundamentally different manipulator kinematics, enabling dexterous operation in environments where conventional industrial robot architectures encounter significant constraints.

Our engineering activities include:

- Robotic kinematics, motion planning and control
- ROS 2 and MoveIt integration
- Manipulation in confined and obstructed workspaces
- Nuclear, aerospace and advanced manufacturing applications
- Adaptive robotic autonomy and modular embodiment

Our public GitHub presence provides information about our engineering activities. Proprietary development repositories remain private.
